About Me

I am Roland, an undergraduate researcher working at the intersection of computer vision, imaging inverse problems, and efficient deep learning architectures. I am currently pursuing a B.S. in Data Science and Programming at the Indian Institute of Technology, Madras alongside a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Sikkim Manipal Institute of Technology, Majitar.

My work spans physics-informed generative models for underwater image restoration, geometric residual learning with state-space models (Mamba) for 3D medical image segmentation, and learned regularization for imaging inverse problems. I care about architectures that are not just accurate but lightweight, interpretable, and deployable in the real world — from AUVs navigating turbid water to clinical segmentation pipelines.

Core Research Themes

  • Imaging Inverse Problems: Bilevel learned regularization — TV and Fields-of-Experts regularizers, implicit-function-theorem gradients, and input-convex neural network (ICNN) regularizers benchmarked on CT and MRI reconstruction/segmentation.
  • Physics-Informed Underwater Vision: Lightweight GANs (9.1M-parameter Turbid-GAN with Triplet Attention) for real-time enhancement of turbid underwater imagery, enabling a 234× improvement in ORB feature matching for AUV visual odometry.
  • State Space Models & Geometric Residual Learning: Integrating Deep Delta Learning (DDL) modules into U-Mamba architectures for 3D medical image segmentation (ACDC, Synapse), with L2-normalized Softplus-constrained weights and β-gate visualization for layer-wise interpretability.
  • Low-Light & Degraded-Scene Vision: Facial emotion recognition and object detection in nighttime conditions using CLAHE, diffusion models, Zero-DCE++, YOLOv8, and fine-tuned CNN backbones.
  • Autonomous Inspection Systems: Real-time detection and tracking (YOLOv8 + DeepSORT) for underwater pipeline inspection and critical infrastructure monitoring.
